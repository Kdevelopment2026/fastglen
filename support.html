Fastglen — Support

Questions or problems? Email kfashola@googlemail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.

I forgot to start the timer.

Start it, then tap Edit start time and set when you actually stopped eating.

I forgot to end my fast.

After a very long fast the app asks "Still fasting?". Tap Set when I ate to record the real end time.

How do I change my rhythm?

Open Plans and pick one. Changing it mid-fast updates the running fast's goal. Settings → Retake the welcome questions suggests one for you.

I bought Plus but it's locked.

Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

Where is the Lock Screen countdown?

It comes with Plus. Start a fast and lock your iPhone. Make sure Live Activities are on in Settings → Fastglen. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours; open Fastglen and the countdown starts again.
