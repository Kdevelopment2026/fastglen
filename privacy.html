Fastglen — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Fastglen does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your fasts, your chosen rhythm, your answers to the welcome questions and your settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

The optional Fastglen Plus purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Health information

Fastglen is a timer and gives general information, not medical advice. It does not read or write Apple Health data.
